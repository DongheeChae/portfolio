Efferocytosis of cytokine pre-licensed MSCs

Ankrum Lab, University of Iowa · PI: James Ankrum · Aug 2025 – May 2026
First author · under review

Showed that cytokine pre-licensing of MSCs elicits the efferocytic response of primary human monocytes, with downstream suppression of T-cell proliferation. I validated the five-colour phenotyping panel, quantified secreted TNF-α, IFN-γ, IL-10 and IL-4 by ELISA, and led manuscript preparation.

Testing whether the immunosuppressive phenotype of efferocytic macrophages propagates to bystander macrophages through exosome-mediated transfer, by contact co-culture and by treating naïve macrophages with PL-MAC-derived exosomes.
